Campaign Insights
1

Call Volume Momentum Building

Total outbound calls climbed to 217 this week, up 7.96% from 201 the previous week. This 16-call increase reflects growing campaign momentum and stronger daily dial activity. Maintaining this trajectory positions the team to surpass the 220-call mark next week, building a healthy top-of-funnel pipeline for Edge Medical Solutions.

2

Connected Rate Dip Needs Attention

Connected calls dropped from 49 to 46, a 6.12% decline, pulling the connected call rate down from 24.38% to 21.20%. Despite higher call volume, fewer prospects answered. This may indicate timing misalignment with the professional training and coaching audience — adjusting call windows to mid-morning or post-lunch slots could improve contact rates with Matthew Britland's target decision-makers.

3

Callback Surge Signals Strong Interest

Callbacks surged by 200%, jumping from just 2 to 6. This is a standout metric this week — tripling callback volume suggests that voicemail and follow-up messaging is resonating well with prospects. Each callback represents a warm re-engagement opportunity and should be prioritised as high-conversion touchpoints in the coming week.

4

Appointment Conversion Improving

Appointments rose from 3 to 4, a 33.33% increase, and the appointment-to-connected ratio improved from 6.12% to 8.70%. This means the team is converting a higher share of conversations into booked meetings — a sign that objection-handling and value proposition delivery for Edge Medical Solutions' professional training services are sharpening.

5

Strategic Focus for Week Ahead

The campaign is showing a positive quality-over-quantity trend: fewer connections but better conversion. Next week's priority should be a dual focus — recapture the 24%+ connected rate by refining call timing and contact data quality, while maintaining the improved 8.70% appointment conversion. With 6 callbacks queued and 4 appointments booked, the pipeline entering the new week is the strongest yet.
